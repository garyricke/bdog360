01 · Strategic Thesis

The fastest way to lose is to outbid National Debt Relief.

Bulldog 360 is a 12-year-old boutique with one to ten employees, a static brochure website, and an owner whose résumé bridges B2B lead generation and consumer debt settlement. It is structurally incapable of winning a paid-search auction against publicly-marketed B2C providers paying $80–120 per click for "debt relief" terms.

It does not have to. The competitor map has a gap in the middle: B2B lead vendors (Pearl Lemon, Leads Warehouse, McGRAW) sell data but don't run consumer funnels; B2C providers (NDR, Freedom, Accredited) run consumer funnels but don't sell their stack to other agencies. The most cost-efficient channel — hybrid direct-mail → IP-retargeting with a 24–48 hour postcard drop on exited web traffic — is well-documented but not owned by any single player at scale.

The plan in one sentence: spend three months and roughly $55K converting bdog360.com from a brochure into a calculator-driven conversion engine with Forth CRM behind it, then deploy a $22.5K/month direct-mail + display-retargeting program against credit-bureau-prescreened lists, and scale to $43K/month only after blended CPA prints below $100.

✦

The bet

Search CPA has compounded ~13.5% per year since 2020 and is structurally inflating further. Display + direct-mail-retargeting CPA has stayed near $57. Every dollar shifted from paid search into the hybrid loop is a dollar earning a ~30% lower acquisition cost — and a dollar that compounds Bulldog's existing direct-mail competency rather than a new core skill.

03 · Competitive Landscape

Two camps. One empty seat in the middle.

The competitor set splits cleanly into B2B data and lead vendors on one side, and scaled B2C debt-settlement brands on the other. No major player owns the hybrid acquisition stack — the gap Bulldog 360 should occupy.

Pearl Lemon Leads

B2B Agency

Intent-verified debt consolidation leads, sold per-lead.

Channels · SEO · cold email · cold calling · pay-per-lead

Strengths

  • Strong SEO authority in the debt-lead category.
  • Multi-channel outbound (email + phone) creates redundancy.
  • Pay-per-lead pricing aligns vendor incentive with client outcome.
  • Reports lead-to-sale conversion at the 8–12% category benchmark.

Weaknesses

  • Pure B2B — no consumer brand or end-customer relationship.
  • No direct-mail or retargeting infrastructure.
  • Quality depends entirely on outbound team skill.
  • Cannot close the loop on prospects that drop out of their funnel.

vs Bulldog 360

  • They sell raw leads; Bulldog 360 owns the entire funnel from first touch through enrollment.
  • Bulldog has 12 years of direct-mail competency Pearl Lemon lacks.
  • More of a potential customer/partner than a head-to-head competitor.

What Bulldog should do

  • Don't compete on raw lead supply — it's a race to the bottom.
  • Position one layer above: buy or generate leads, then close them through Bulldog's calculator funnel.
  • Borrow the pay-per-outcome pricing model for white-label customers in Stage 3.

The Leads Warehouse

Data Vendor

Aged, real-time, and call-transfer consumer debt leads across price tiers.

Channels · direct mail · RVM · real-time form fills

Strengths

  • Massive volume across all price tiers — $0.10 aged to $75 live call transfer.
  • Multi-source sourcing reduces dependency on any one channel.
  • Plug-and-play supplier — easy to test in small batches before scaling.

Weaknesses

  • Pure data with zero execution — they don't qualify or close.
  • Aged lead quality is unpredictable; conversion varies wildly.
  • No proprietary relationship with the end consumer — every touch is cold.

vs Bulldog 360

  • Supplier, not a competitor.
  • Their data converts at higher rates inside Bulldog's hybrid funnel than they can monetize directly.
  • Bulldog already budgeted $5K/mo for lead purchases in Stage 2 — they're the target vendor.

What Bulldog should do

  • Buy aged leads as dialer fuel at $0.10–$1.00 each to keep reps busy.
  • Test real-time form leads at $10–$60 against the calculator landing page.
  • Don't build a competing data-vendor business — partner instead.

Direct consumer debt settlement at scale — the category's most visible brand.

Three layers of unfair advantage.

Bulldog 360 doesn't need a breakthrough — it needs to combine assets it already has into a system competitors can't easily replicate.

A

Mail + IP loop

Closed-loop hybrid

Existing direct-mail competency feeds programmatic IP-display retargeting. The 24–48 hour postcard drop on exited web traffic is documented as one of the highest-converting plays in the category — and no major B2C provider runs it because they lack the print infrastructure.

B

Operator domain

Founder bridge

Tad Piron's prior representation of debt-settlement entities creates a compliance and operator network that scaled B2C marketers don't have. That regulatory fluency matters most where Google & FTC compliance gate channel access.

C

White-label upside

Productize the stack

Once the on-site funnel + Forth CRM + retargeting stack is operating, the same infrastructure can be licensed to smaller debt-settlement agencies as a turn-key acquisition engine — a SaaS-adjacent recurring revenue line that no current competitor offers.

Pillar C — white-label productization — is a 12-to-18 month destination, not a Month 1 deliverable. 06 · Execution Plan

Three stages. One sequence. Twelve months.

Each stage gates the next. Don't scale paid media until the funnel converts; don't productize until the funnel scales.

Stage 1 · Months 1–3

Foundation Sprint: build the conversion engine

Q1 2026 · 90 days

~$55K One-time investment

Build

Replace the static bdog360.com brochure with a mid-range client portal: interactive multi-step debt savings calculator, trust badges and authority signaling, Forth CRM integration with soft-pull credit checks, and Forth Pay escrow account setup. This is the conversion engine — every dollar of paid media after this point flows through it.

  • Multi-step calculator validating debt >$10K, creditor count, employment, and bankruptcy status — same pre-qualification structure used by Freedom and NDR.
  • Forth CRM + Forth Credit + Forth Pay integration ($5K–15K dev + ~$1.2K–3.6K/yr license) — instant soft-pull credit verification, FTC-compliant escrow setup, automated workflow.
  • Trust architecture — BBB / ACDR / Trustpilot widgets, performance-based fee disclosure, "no upfront fees" messaging at every scroll depth.
  • PURL infrastructure so direct-mail prospects land on personalized pages (JohnDoe.bdog360.com) that pre-fill in the calculator.
  • Credologi DSL integration for downstream program-funding handoff once enrollment closes.

Stage 2 · Months 4–9

Acquisition Engine: feed the funnel, conservatively

Q2–Q3 2026 · 6 months

$22.5K Per month · conservative

Run

Activate channels in the order that compounds Bulldog's existing strengths. Start with prescreened direct mail to credit-bureau-qualified households, layer in programmatic display retargeting against site visitors, and use paid search only on high-intent bottom-funnel queries. Hold $22.5K/month until blended CPA prints under $100 for two consecutive months.

  • Prescreened direct mail against credit-bureau data — debt >$10K, high utilization, payment-strain indicators. PURL + QR + BRE response paths.
  • Programmatic display + paid social retargeting ($10K/mo) — sequential creative against calculator abandons. Postcard drop within 24–48 hours.
  • Bottom-funnel paid search ($1.5K/mo cap) — branded and creditor-name queries only. Skip top-of-funnel keyword inflation.
  • Lead-vendor supplement ($5K/mo) — mix of real-time form leads ($10–60) and aged lists ($0.10–1.00) to keep dialers busy.
  • Agency retainer ($5K/mo) — SEO + PPC management + creative production for 4–8 compliance-checked articles/month and ad creative.

Stage 3 · Months 10–12

Scale & Productize: double the spend, package the stack

Q4 2026 · 3 months

$43K Per month · aggressive

Scale

Once Stage 2 prints a blended CPA below $100 and reps are running at 3–5 enrollments per week, lift monthly spend to $43K and begin packaging the stack as a white-label offering. Two parallel motions: double down on what works, and build the asset that generates recurring revenue.

  • Paid media to $15K/mo and lead vendors to $10K/mo — expand into live-call transfers ($60–75/transfer) as enrollment capacity grows.
  • Agency retainer to $11K/mo — add high-authority backlink acquisition ($500–1,000 per niche edit) and specialist debt-finance content writers ($450–1,500/article).
  • SaaS & tech stack to $4K/mo — full Forth CRM seat licenses for an expanded rep team, advanced soft-credit API usage, premium hosting.
  • Security & compliance to $3K/mo — annual penetration testing, compliance legal audits, AI security scanning.
  • White-label productization — codify the Foundation Sprint deliverable into a sellable package for smaller agencies (the Empowerment pillar from the Brand Guide).
✦

Gating discipline

Stage 2 does not begin until Stage 1's calculator + CRM are live in production. Stage 3 does not begin until Stage 2 prints CPA < $100 for two consecutive months. If Stage 2 misses that threshold, do not scale — diagnose the funnel before adding spend.

09 · Risk Register

What could break this plan — and what we'd do about it.

Every assumption above has a counterfactual. The five most material risks, with the pre-decided response.

Click any risk to expand the response

R1

Google policy gate on debt-services advertising

U.S. Google Ads restricts debt-settlement advertising to approved non-profit credit-counseling agencies. Bulldog 360 may not qualify for the certification required to bid on consumer-facing debt queries at all.

Response: Treat paid search as bonus, not baseline. The plan already de-prioritizes top-of-funnel search. Pursue certification on a parallel track but do not gate Stage 2 on it.

R2

Foundation Sprint overruns

Mid-range portal builds routinely overshoot timeline and budget. A 6-month, $150K outcome would push Stage 2 deep into Q3 and starve the back half of 2026 of acquisition volume.

Response: Fixed-scope contract with the build agency. MVP cuts: defer multi-role portals and AI features. Ship a single-role consumer calculator with Forth integration on a 90-day clock; iterate after.

R3

Direct-mail compliance & deliverability

Credit-bureau prescreening data carries FCRA "firm offer of credit" obligations and tight creative-review windows. Errors trigger enforcement risk and bureau cutoff.

Response: Run all prescreened mail copy through Tad Piron's legal network before drop. Use a credit-bureau-approved mail-house with a documented compliance pipeline. Sample-test 10% before national mail rounds.

R4

CPA fails to break $100

If Stage 2 retargeting underperforms category benchmarks, blended CPA stays above $100 and Stage 3 gating triggers indefinite pause — Bulldog stays at $22.5K/month without scaling.

Response: Pause spend after 60 days under-threshold rather than burn budget. Diagnose: funnel UX, mailer creative, retarget cadence. Bring in a category-specific optimization sprint before re-attempting.

R5

SaaS productization gets ahead of operations

Selling the stack to other agencies before Bulldog's own funnel is proven creates a credibility liability and operational drag — the support burden of multiple white-label tenants without a battle-tested core.

Response: Delay external white-label sales until Bulldog 360 has run its own funnel at scale for 90+ days. The Brand Guide already flags this as a future-state pillar; Stage 3 packaging work is internal until Year 2.
