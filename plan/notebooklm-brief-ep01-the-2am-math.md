## Key facts & numbers (illustrative example)

Use this worked example as the centerpiece. Present these as an *illustration*, not a quote or guarantee.

- **Balance:** $8,000 on one credit card.
- **APR:** 24% (a common rate in the current market).
- **If you pay only the minimum:** it takes roughly **27 years** to clear the card, and you pay **more than $11,000 in interest** — on top of the original $8,000.
- **Why:** the minimum payment is calculated to keep the account "current" while barely reducing the principal. Early on, the large majority of each payment goes to interest.
- **A faster path:** debt-settlement programs negotiate the *balance itself* down, and typically aim to resolve enrolled unsecured debt in **24–48 months**.

## The three paths (keep this light — it's a teaser for Episode 2)

- **Consolidation** — roll balances into one new loan/payment. Can lower the rate, but it's still a loan: the full principal remains, and it only helps if you don't run the cards back up.
- **Settlement** — negotiate with creditors to resolve the debt for less than the full balance; one structured monthly program payment. This is Bulldog 360's core service.
- **Bankruptcy** — a legal reset; powerful but with the longest, deepest credit consequences. Usually the last resort, not the first.
- **The throughline:** the right move attacks the principal and has a finish line measured in months, not decades.
