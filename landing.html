Good questions

What you're probably wondering

Will this hurt my credit?

Checking your estimate has no impact on your credit score. Enrolling in a debt-resolution program can affect your credit during the program, but many clients see their score recover as balances are resolved and utilization drops.

How much does it cost?

You pay nothing upfront. Our performance-based fee is charged only after a debt is successfully settled, in full compliance with the FTC's Telemarketing Sales Rule. Your monthly deposits accumulate in a dedicated account that you own and control.

What kinds of debt qualify?

Unsecured debts — credit cards, personal loans, medical bills, and most collections — typically qualify. Secured debts such as mortgages and auto loans, plus federal student loans, generally do not.

How long does the program take?

Most clients complete their program in 24 to 48 months, depending on enrolled balances and the monthly amount they can comfortably set aside. Your specialist will map out a realistic timeline before you commit to anything.
